KC85 Tape Recorder

Record and decode audio from KC85 tapes into digital tape files.

How to Use:

  • Click "Start Recording" to begin
  • Play the KC85 tape audio (from cassette player or emulator)
  • Click "Stop & Decode" when the tape finishes
  • The decoder will analyze the audio and extract the data
  • Download the decoded tape file

Technical Details:

  • Pulse-length decoding (short ~0.5ms, long ~1ms)
  • Automatic pilot tone and sync detection
  • Parity and checksum validation
  • Supports standard KC85 tape formats (.KCC, .TAP, .SSS)
